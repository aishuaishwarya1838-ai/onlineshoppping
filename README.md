# Online Shopping System Project – Complete Information

An Online Shopping System is a web-based application that allows customers to purchase products through the internet without visiting a physical store. Customers can browse products, add items to their shopping cart, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

It is a good project for college students, especially for BCA, BSc Computer Science, B.Tech, MCA, and diploma courses.

## 1. Project title

Online Shopping Management System

## 2. Project abstract

The Online Shopping System is designed to make shopping easy, convenient, and faster for customers. This system provides an online platform where customers can view different products, check product details, compare prices, and purchase products.

The application has two main users: Admin and Customer.

- Admin: Manages products, categories, customer details, orders, and payments.
- Customer: Registers, logs in, searches for products, adds products to the cart, and places orders.

The main purpose of this project is to reduce manual work and provide a simple, secure, and user-friendly shopping experience.

## 3. Objectives of the project

1. To develop an online shopping website.
2. To provide easy access to products.
3. To save customers' time.
4. To allow customers to purchase products online.
5. To manage product and customer information.
6. To provide secure login and registration.
7. To maintain order and payment details.
8. To make shopping convenient and efficient.
